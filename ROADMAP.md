## Anonymous reporting: before going live
- [ ] Choose an Intermountain-approved way to receive reports that records no names, sign-ins, emails or IP addresses
- [ ] Decide who reviews reports (ideally including someone outside residents' direct supervision, e.g., the DIO / GME office)
- [ ] Move the reviewer inbox off this device to a secured, access-controlled location
- [ ] Set a response standard (e.g., acknowledge within 2 business days) and an escalation path for safety issues
- [ ] Put the reporting process and non-retaliation statement in the resident handbook
- [ ] Agree how reports are summarized for the PEC / APE without identifying anyone
- [ ] Introduce it at orientation; post the QR code in workrooms and on badge cards
- [ ] Run an end-to-end test before the first residents arrive
